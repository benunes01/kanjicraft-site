About

KanjiCraft is a Japanese kanji learning app featuring:

  • Interactive lessons with mnemonics and vocabulary
  • Spaced repetition (SM-2) for efficient memorization
  • Handwriting practice with on-device AI recognition
  • Quizzes, XP, streaks, and achievements
  • JLPT N5 to N1 coverage (2,000+ kanji)
  • Available in Portuguese, English, and Spanish

AI Disclaimer

Some educational content in KanjiCraft — including kanji meanings, mnemonics, example vocabulary, and stroke data — may have been generated or curated with the assistance of AI tools. While we strive for accuracy, language learning content may contain errors. KanjiCraft is a supplementary study tool and should not be considered the sole reference for JLPT preparation or academic purposes.

Handwriting recognition uses an on-device machine learning model. No writing data is sent to external servers.

Free vs Pro PRO

Free: JLPT N5 (~100 kanji), all core features, ads between sessions.

Pro: All JLPT levels (N5-N1, 2,000+ kanji), no ads, advanced statistics.
