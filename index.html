What it is

KanjiCraft teaches the 2,202 JLPT kanji, from N5 to N1, with meanings, memory hints and example words in your language.

  • A daily session: reviews at the right time and new kanji at your pace (FSRS spaced repetition).
  • Lessons with animated stroke order, readings, audio, a memory hint and common words.
  • Writing: trace the kanji, then write it from memory, with stroke-by-stroke feedback computed on your device.
  • Quick training and a list of the kanji you forget most.
  • Streaks with freezes, achievements and progress by level.
  • Works offline. An account is optional and backs up and syncs your progress.
  • In Portuguese, English, Spanish and Indonesian.

Free and Pro PRO

Free: all of N5 (103 kanji), the “At the restaurant” theme, reviews always included and, after N5, one extra N4 lesson a day by watching an ad. The free version shows ads.

Pro: all 2,202 kanji from N5 to N1, all 8 everyday themes, no ads and advanced stats (forecast and retention). Prices are shown in the app, in your store’s currency.

About the content

Kanji, readings and words come from open dictionaries (KANJIDIC2 and JMdict), and stroke order comes from KanjiVG. Meanings, memory hints and translations were written with the help of AI tools and may contain errors. Found one? Let us know through support.

  • Menulis: tebalkan kanji, lalu tulis dari ingatan, dengan koreksi per goresan yang dihitung di perangkatmu.

  • Bisa dipakai tanpa internet. Akun bersifat opsional dan berguna untuk mencadangkan serta menyinkronkan progres.
